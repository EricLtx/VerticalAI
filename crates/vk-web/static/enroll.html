This makes Windows Hello (or the passkey on your phone) the device that speaks for you on this node: every approval of a task's artefact will ask it to sign, and nothing else will be accepted as a human decision.

Your browser will ask which authenticator to use and then for your PIN, fingerprint or face. The kernel keeps only the public key, under a device id it will show you.
